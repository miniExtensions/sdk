About this example

This is a custom application, with a small field renderer you can replace. miniExtensions validates permissions and Form rules on the server. Your application owns its layout, conditional presentation, CAPTCHA integration, and visitor session storage.

Writes are never retried automatically. If a request is cancelled or loses its connection, reload and inspect the records before deciding to submit again.
